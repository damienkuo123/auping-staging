                      常見問題

                      The Auping Auronde is available in widths ranging from 80 to 200 cm and lengths of 200, 210 or 220 cm. The bed frame height is 34.5 cm as standard, and can be increased to 41.5 cm or 48 cm.

                      Depending on the selected bed frame height, the sleeping height is 49 cm, 56 cm or 62 cm when combined with an Auping Evolve mattress of 22 cm.

                      The lowest version provides 17 cm of clearance underneath the bed, offering space for storage boxes or containers.

                      The delivery time for your bed depends on the specific configuration you choose. On average, delivery takes between 3 and 8 weeks. This is because each bed is made to order, ensuring it is tailored to your preferences.

                      You can choose from 10 different lacquer colours and 87 fabrics in a variety of textures. In our online configurator, you can explore all colours and fabrics and instantly see what your favourite combination looks like.

                      When you order an Auping Auronde, we ensure a hassle-free delivery and professional installation. This service is included as standard with your order and costs €99.

                      Here's how it works:

                      1. Personal delivery appointment
                      After placing your order, we'll contact you to arrange a delivery date that fits your schedule.

                      2. Delivery & assembly
                      Our experienced team will deliver your bed directly to your bedroom and assemble it carefully in the desired location.

                      Old mattress collection
                      Need to get rid of your old mattress? Choose our collection service and we'll make sure it is recycled responsibly. Good for you and good for the planet.

                      3. After-sales service
                      Do you have any questions after delivery or would you like to make an adjustment? Our customer service team is happy to help and ensure your sleeping comfort is exactly as you want it to be.

                      Auping offers a 5-year full warranty on the Auronde. This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship.

                      Learn more about our warranty. 

                      Not completely satisfied with your order? When you purchase online from Auping, you have a 30-day return period. This also applies to online purchases of the Essential bed. View our exchange and return policy for full details.
