                      常見問題

                      The delivery time for an Essential bed depends on the configuration you choose. On average, delivery takes between 3 and 8 weeks. This is because every Essential is made to order, ensuring it is tailored to your preferences.

                      When you order an Auping Essential, we make sure your bed is delivered and installed with the utmost care. This service is included as a standard part of your order and costs €99. Here's how it works:

                      1. Personal delivery appointment
                      After placing your order, we'll contact you to schedule a delivery date. We'll work around your availability to find a time that suits you best.

                      2. Professional delivery and assembly
                      Our experienced delivery team will bring your bed to your home and assemble it with care and expertise. They'll make sure everything is set up perfectly in your bedroom.

                      Old mattress collection
                      Need to get rid of your old mattress? We can take it with us and recycle it responsibly, helping to reduce waste and contribute to a more sustainable future.

                      3. After-delivery support
                      If you have any questions after delivery or would like to make an adjustment, our customer service team is here to help. We'll do everything we can to ensure your sleep experience is exactly as it should be.
