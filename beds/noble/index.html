                      Noble

                      低調奢華

                      Auping Noble 是 Johan van Hengel 為 Auping 設計的優雅床架，以低調方式呈現奢華。寬闊實心橡木平台與圓潤邊角構成簡潔而有力量的外型，並在 Deventer 以精選自歐洲森林的永續橡木手工製作。

                      設計您的 Noble

                       

                      色彩與材質

                      Noble 木材提供六種染色：

                      • 柔白橡木
                      • 原色橡木
                      • 咖啡棕橡木
                      • 巧克力棕橡木
                      • 深藍橡木
                      • 午夜黑橡木

                      The headboard and lounge pillows are upholstered with one of the fabrics from the Auping fabric collection. The Auping fabric collection consists of five groups with 15 fabrics in no less than 95 different colours. Inspired by what we see around us in nature, these colours are all equally soft,calm and applicable in every bedroom. Want to add even more colours?  This can be done by adding textiles and accessories in different textures and colours. 

                      神清氣爽地醒來

                      Auping Noble 可搭配所有 Auping 床墊與床底；若需要協助，可至門市由專員依身形提供建議。   

                      預約諮詢

                       

                      Johan van Hengel

                      The design also finds it origin nearby. Rotterdam based Johan van Hengel creates beautiful items using rich materials, combined with the user in mind. Johan worked, amongst others, for Muuto, Hay en Montis.

                      "During the design process of this bed, I focused on modest luxury, use and comfortable security. For me, luxury is also in the use of high-quality and natural materials. Hence the choice for solid oak, which gives a nice sturdy and qualitative look and also lasts a long time. The understated shape in combination with the warmth of fabric, large lounge cushions and a headboard across the width creates a luxurious, generous and timeless bed".

                      認識 Noble 設計師

                      倚靠大型軟墊自在放鬆

                      Different from our other beds, the Auping Noble has two big pillows attached to the headboard. They are as wide as the mattress you choose, so you're super comfy and well rested. Of course you can design the pillows to your own taste. Choose the same colour as the headboard if you like, or make it a bit more adventurous with contrasting colours.  

                      Legs in a lovely round shape

                      The legs of Noble contribute to the soft look of the design. They come in a round shape, but they're not completely round. As a nice surprise, they have a rounded square, which gives it that extra touch.

                      打造夢想中的 Noble

                      In our online configurator you can put together your Noble completely according to your wishes. Choose the colour of your bed frame, headboard, accessories and the ideal size for your bedroom. Prefer some help with designing your bed? Then make a store appointment. Our sleep experts are happy to help you putting together your dream bed.

                      前往自行搭配   預約諮詢

                      Tables designed to service you

                      This is also our first bed where the bedtables are part of the design of the bed. De legs are in the middle of the tables, which gives it a stylish finish. This way, not only a harmonious look appears but it also gives to a place for your breakfast or favorite book.

                      Royal headbord for that safeness and security

                      The elegant en wide headbord is attached with a hidden support to the bed. Thanks to the royal size, it gives you that ultimate safe and secure feeling.

                      Sustainable design from solid oak

                      Auping Noble is made of solid oak. That requires real craftsmanship and care. Wood is a sustainable and natural material, it is a renewable resource. We choose our wood from forests in Europe where replanting takes place. Because the bed is made of high quality solid oak, it will last for generations, which makes Auping Noble a sustainable choice, that will last for years.

                      Award-winning Design

                      Auping Noble has been awarded the Archiproducts Design Award 2023. 

                       

                      The Archiproducts Design Awards are an initiative of Archiproducts. With more than 230,000 products, 3,500 design brands and 2.7 million users, Archiproducts is the worlds’ largest information and inspiration network for architecture and design.
