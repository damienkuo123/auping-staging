                      Criade

                      以堅固鋼材為核心的精緻床組

                      Criade 以纖細線條呈現精緻床組外型，鋼製框架與水平線條形成獨特識別。您可自由選擇布料與框架顏色，營造專屬臥室氛圍。

                      打造您的 Criade

                       

                      色彩與材質

                      鋼材與布料 
                      Criade 鋼製框架可選十種粉體烤漆色，並提供多達九十五種布料，分為五個布料群組。 

                      • Fabric group A: These are basic fabrics in uni colours with subtle structures. All three fabrics, Grid, Peach and Salt are made of polyester. 

                      • Substance group B are substances with special structures. The fabrics Points, Maze and Dew all have a high cuddliness content.

                      • In fabric group C you will find two warm, rich and graphic fabrics: Facet and Reflect. 

                      • Fabric group D consists of 3 premium woolen fabrics, all three from the Danish fabric manufacturer Kvadrat: Clara, Remix and Re-wool. 

                      • Fabric group E contains three luxurious and exclusive fabrics, Plecto, Sprinkles and Jaali. These are beautiful to combine with the other fabrics from the Auping range and give a soothing feeling of luxury in the bedroom. 

                      床頭板與床尾板
                      Criade 提供四款床頭板與三款床尾板，皆於 Deventer 縫製工坊手工包覆。 

                      • Plain: a rectangular headboard with a sleek, bright design (there is also a Plain footboard) 

                      • Bend: a two-piece curved headboard with rounded details (there's also a Bend footboard) 

                      • Square: a high headboard, quilted and classically finished with round buttons (there is also a Square footboard) 

                      • Cushion: a soft and somewhat looser upholstered headboard finished with elongated, metal rods that always have the same color as the frame of the box 

                      神清氣爽地醒來

                      On the lower part of a box spring, the box, is a box spring mattress. These matching mattresses are supplied as standard with our box springs.  On top of that comes a mattress topper. In the store you will receive advice on which box spring mattress best supports your body. All Auping box spring mattresses have been developed with care and ensure a good night's sleep.

                      探索 Criade

                      用心設計

                      The Criade is a richly filled box spring: the box contains a mesh base and pocket springs. The Auping mesh base is eighty percent open and therefore the box spring ventilates very well. The pocket springs in both the box spring and the mattress ensure that you can easily turn at night and have good support during your sleep.

                      The Criade is a refined box spring with slender lines. The steel frame gives the Criade its unique signature where the horizontal line carries the design.

                      Auping Design Studio - Auping Criade 設計團隊

                      打造夢想中的 Criade

                      In our online configurator you can put together your Criade completely to your liking. Choose the color of your bed frame, the headboard, the legs, the bedside tables and the size that fits your bedroom. Prefer some help with designing your box spring? Then make a shopping appointment. Our sleep experts are happy to help you put together the box spring of your dreams.
