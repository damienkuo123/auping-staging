                      The delivery time for an Auping Original box spring depends on the specific configuration you choose. On average, delivery takes between 3 and 8 weeks. This is because every Original box spring is made to order, ensuring it is tailored to your preferences.

                      When you order an Auping Original Boxspring, we make sure your bed is delivered and installed with the utmost care. This service is included as a standard part of your order and costs €99. Here's how it works:

                      1. Personal delivery appointment
                      After placing your order, we'll contact you to schedule a delivery date. We'll work around your availability to find a time that suits you best.

                      2. Professional delivery and assembly
                      Our experienced delivery team will bring your bed to your home and assemble it with care and expertise. They'll make sure everything is set up perfectly in your bedroom.

                      Old mattress collection
                      Need to get rid of your old mattress? We can take it with us and recycle it responsibly, helping to reduce waste and contribute to a more sustainable future.

                      3. After-delivery support
                      If you have any questions after delivery or would like to make an adjustment, our customer service team is here to help. We'll do everything we can to ensure your sleep experience is exactly as it should be.

                      A bed consists of a bed frame, a slatted base and a mattress. A box spring, on the other hand, is a complete sleep system made up of an upholstered spring base, an upholstered mattress and, optionally, a topper. This provides added comfort, improved ventilation and a more luxurious look and feel.

                      Yes, the Auping Original box spring is on display in all Auping Stores. You can explore different configurations and fabrics, and experience the comfort for yourself.

                      A box spring mattress is typically upholstered and specifically designed for use on a box spring. Upholstered box spring mattresses are usually paired with a topper.

                      A white mattress is a standard, non-upholstered mattress, often used on a slatted base. A white mattress can also be used on a box spring without a topper.

                      A topper provides an extra layer of comfort and can make your bed feel softer or firmer, depending on your preference. Without a topper, you sleep directly on the mattress, which may result in a firmer feel. The right choice ultimately depends on your personal sleep preferences.

                      An Auping Original box spring has an average lifespan of 15 to 20 years under normal use. Its lifespan may vary depending on maintenance and the quality of the mattresses and toppers used. An Auping mattress typically lasts around 10 years under normal use.

                      Auping provides a 5-year warranty on the Original box spring. This warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship.

                      Yes, the Auping Original box spring is available in both flat and electrically adjustable versions. The adjustable models offer added comfort, allowing you to adjust the head and/or foot section to your preferred position.

                      When choosing a box spring, consider the following:

                      • The type of mattress and the level of firmness
                      • The quality of the springs (pocket springs often provide the highest level of comfort)
                      • Ventilation and moisture regulation
                      • Optional features such as electric adjustability
                      • The design and fabric options that suit your interior

                      The Auping Original box spring is crafted with care and craftsmanship in Deventer, the Netherlands. Auping is known for its sustainable production processes and attention to detail, both of which contribute to the exceptional quality of its products.
