                      需要協助嗎？

                      對網路商店的訂購流程有疑問嗎？我們整理了常見問題；若仍找不到答案，歡迎聯絡客服。

                      Our customer service is available Monday to Friday from 8.30 am to 5 pm.
                      You can reach us at klantenservice@auping.nl or call 0570-681820.

                      訂購方式

                      如何將商品加入購物車？
                      Select your preferred items in the product section and click on the blue 'Add to cart' button.
                      You will now be directed to your shopping cart overview. You can always view the contents of your shopping cart by clicking on the 'Shopping Cart' icon at the top of the website.

                      可以修改購物車內容嗎？
                      That is possible. In the shopping basket overview you can still remove products or change the number of products by clicking on the + and - or 'remove this item'.

                      Would you like to add more products to your shopping cart?
                      Then click on 'Continue shopping' to continue shopping.

                      折扣碼要在哪裡輸入？
                      In the shopping cart overview you can click on the arrow next to the discount code. You can then add your discount code in the field and click on apply discount code.

                      My discount code does not work.
                      Unable to add the code or is the code not working? Please contact our customer service.

                      如何結帳？
                      If you wish to checkout the products in your shopping cart, click on the blue "Proceed to checkout" button. You will automatically be directed to the ordering process. Follow the steps to successfully complete your shopping cart and confirm your order.

                      Can I have my order delivered somewhere other than my billing address?
                      That is possible. By default, your billing address is used as the shipping address. Would you like a different shipping address? In 'My Auping', you can change your delivery address in the address book. Enter the details of the desired delivery address here and you will receive your order at this address.

                      Once you have paid for your order, you can no longer change the delivery address.
                      Mocht je het verkeerde afleveradres hebben opgegeven, neem dan zo snel mogelijk contact op met onze klantenservice. We kijken dan of het nog mogelijk is om het afleveradres aan te passen.

                      可以更改或取消訂單嗎？
                      If you want to change or cancel your order, we advise you to contact the customer service. Unfortunately, we cannot make any changes to your order once it has been dispatched.

                      I have not received an order and payment confirmation?
                      Please contact our customer service, we will investigate what went wrong and send you the order and payment confirmation again.

                      I have not received a shipping confirmation or an invoice?
                      Please contact our customer service, we will investigate what went wrong and send you the shipping confirmation and invoice again.

                      在 Auping 購物安全嗎？
                      When sending sensitive information, such as your payment details, we use a secure internet connection. When using a secure internet connection, the URL www. auping.com/en is preceded by https:// instead of the normal http://. The letter 's' stands for 'secure'.

                      We also advise you not to give anyone your customer number, password or other details concerning your Auping account.

                      Cancellation?
                      Once you have paid for an order, you can no longer cancel it. If you have not been able to cancel the order in time, you can return it to our delivery service immediately.

                      可以訂購備用零件嗎？
                      Yes, you can. Various parts such as the gas spring or a pull cord can still be ordered. Contact the Auping shop.
                      Find an Auping Store in your area.
