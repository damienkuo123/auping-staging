Auping duvet covers

Convenient: Insert Flap All Auping duvet covers feature an extra-long insert flap, making it easy to tuck the cover under your mattress. Cold feet are a thing of the past. You can recognize the extra-long insert flap by the length size: 200/220 cm. Sizes of Our Duvet Covers Our duvet cover sizes are tailored to match Auping’s range of duvets and pillows for a perfect fit. In addition, the duvet covers and pillowcases are also suitable for most standard duvets and pillows. Auping duvet covers are available in both single and double sizes: 140 x 200/220 cm including 1 pillowcase of 60 x 70 cm 200 x 200/220 cm including 2 pillowcases of 60 x 70 cm 240 x 200/220 cm including 2 pillowcases of 60 x 70 cm 260 x 200/220 cm including 2 pillowcases of 60 x 70 cm Quality Certifications We value not only sustainability but also the quality of our products. That’s why we devote great attention to this and work with the following labels and institutions to ensure our quality. All Auping duvet covers are certified with the Oeko-Tex Standard 100. This label, issued by the Oeko-Tex Institute, guarantees that the tested and certified textiles are free from harmful substances with respect to human health and environmental impact. It is essentially a safety label for textiles
