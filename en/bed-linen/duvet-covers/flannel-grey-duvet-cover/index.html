There was an error displaying this product. Flannel grey This grey duvet cover, made of flannel, is wonderfully warm for winter. Flannel is a woven fabric, which can be made of cotton or wool. The fabric is brushed after weaving, causing the fibres to stand up. This gives that wonderfully soft layer. Both the front and back of the duvet cover and pillowcase are woven in a timeless herringbone structure. Soft and comfortable, this will get you through the cold nights

Auping duvet covers

Tuck-in flap All Auping duvet covers have an extra-long tuck-in flap across the entire width and on both sides. This allows you to tuck the duvet cover under your mattress. Cold feet will become a thing of the past. You can recognise the extra-long tuck-in flap by the length of the duvet cover: 200/220. Sustainable sleep At Auping, we dream of a well-rested world, one in which we sleep under bedding produced in an eco-friendly way and under good working conditions. All Auping duvet covers, pillowcases, pillows and duvets are OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certified, one of the most famous quality standards for textiles. If a product bears the OEKO-TEX label, then every part of it -from the threads to the buttons- has been tested for substances harmful to human health. In addition, the autumn-winter collection also carries the BCI label, which guarantees sustainable and socially responsible production of bed textiles
