Sustainable choice

Down is a natural product with a very low environmental impact because not much processing is required to make this material ready for use. Moreover, because of its low weight and high insulating power, a down duvet needs half the amount of filling as a polyester duvet. In addition, down is biodegradable and not harmful if it ends up in the environment. Down from discarded pillows and duvets is also easy to recycle. Recycled down therefore has an even lower environmental impact than new (virgin) down. Auping Cloud duvets are RDS-certified

For all seasons

All Auping duvets are available in a 4-season, spring/fall and summer version. Generally speaking, the spring/fall variant (warmth class 3) is a nice duvet for the whole year. If you feel warm in the summer, choose the summer variant (warmth class 4). For the really cold winter nights, you can easily knot both parts together to make a 4-season duvet (warmth class 1)
