Sustainable sleeping At Auping, we dream of a rested world. Of sleeping under wonderful bedding made in an environmentally friendly way and under good working conditions. All Auping duvet covers, pillowcases, pillows and duvets have an OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 certificate, one of the best-known labels for textiles. For a product with this label, every part, from the thread to the button, has been tested for harmful substances for human health
