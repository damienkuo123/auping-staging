The Auronde

is made of extremely strong beech wood and is the basis for this bed. The design was revolutionary in the 1970s and is still timeless today because of its shape and colour. This variation in Pine green with flat mesh base and Pine green details is a real showpiece of the bedroom. The dark colour in the materials gives the Auronde a sleek finish. The Auping Auronde stands for timelessness and will last for generations
