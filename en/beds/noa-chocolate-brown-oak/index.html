The Noa

bed is a true Scandinavian design with its simple shape, clever details and natural materials. This variation in Chocolate brown oak with an open lamellas headboard, 1 motor electric adjustable bed bases and Noa wheels is a real eye-catcher of the bedroom. The Auping Noa stands for peace, comfort and security. Thanks to the use of natural materials and the classic look of the bed, it fits any bedroom
