A 140x200 mattress is the smallest size of a double mattress. It is an ideal mattress for box springs and beds that do not take up too much space in your bedroom. If you like to sleep snugly in one bed with your bed partner or if you prefer to lie wide on your own, a 140x200 mattress is suitable for you. Choose the 140x200 mattress that is right for you To determine which 140x200 mattress is right for you, we look at your build and listen to your personal needs.
