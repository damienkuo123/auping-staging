Auping publiceert CSR Report 2022

About Auping Sustainability 2 minutes 07-07-2023

Making dreams come true

Today, we are delighted to publish the Auping Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Report 2022 with a sense of pride. We provide an overview of the sustainable steps we took in 2022 and share our goals for the future. In 2022, we provided rest to 76,000 people, reduced emissions by 29%, improved our circularity score, and received highly positive feedback from our employees at Auping, with an eNPS (employee Net Promoter Score) of 35 points. In this CSR Report, we are even more specific than in previous years. We not only share insights about the past year but also communicate in detail about our goals for the future. This includes, for example, our ambition to have a fully circular product range by 2030, further reduce our energy consumption, and be an even better employer for our colleagues

Inspiring insights

Since 2020, Auping has been a certified B Corp, demonstrating that we belong to a group of companies with a clear and, above all, measurable focus on people, the environment, and society. We do not create this report about our societal impact out of obligation but rather to provide an inspiring insight into our way of doing business. We showcase the impact we have achieved together with our partners and customers. Because our goals can only be accomplished if we continue to work together and support each other. Only then can we truly bring rest to the world. Read the CSR Report 2022 here
