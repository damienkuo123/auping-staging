Building a Circular Future: Auping and TripleR Redefine Mattress Recycling with Digital Identification Solutions

True circularity means closing the circular loop. Different stakeholders in the ecosystem need different information to make recycling without quality loss possible. Auping and Triple R are collaborating to make this process transparent for everyone. According to European Bedding Industry Association, each year, 30 million mattresses are discarded in Europe, leading to significant CO2 emissions, as most mattresses are difficult to recycle and end up in incinerators. In 2018, Auping launched the world's first circular mattress, designed so that its materials can be infinitely reused. However, circular design alone is not enough to ensure true sustainability; there must be a closed-loop system in place. A closed loop enables high-quality recycling and requires an ecosystem with multiple stakeholders, all of whom must have the right information to minimize environmental impact. Within this ecosystem, returned mattresses are processed, with the polyester being recycled into new fiber. This fiber is then used to create nonwovens for new circular mattresses, thereby reducing waste and promoting sustainability. The business case in the long term lies in the qualitative materials for which demand will increase due to regulations. To inform the stakeholder about the closed loop of the mattress, Auping has joined forces with mattressID ecosystem developed by TripleR, which uses Avery Dennison’s atma.io connected product cloud platform to make mattresses transparent for both consumers and dismantlers. The mattressID will facilitate the creation of this closed loop by providing essential information to all stakeholders involved.
